Quick start

Controls and frequently asked questions

How do I play Poké-Mario?

Click “Play free now” to load the game. Use a keyboard: ← and → to move, Space or ↑ to jump, and X to throw a Poké Ball and start a battle.

Can I play Pokémon games online for free?

Yes. Poké-Mario is a free Pokémon game you can play online in your browser. Jump through varied levels, catch wild Pokémon and fight turn-based battles—no download or account needed.

How do I get through Cucumber Castle (Level 1-7)?

Cucumber Castle is level seven. Jump across the dark castle platforms, collect cucumbers and press G to throw them at pumpkins blocking your way.

Is my progress saved?

Your profile is stored locally in your browser. It does not sync between devices. Clearing browser data also removes local saves.

How do I catch wild Pokémon in Poké-Mario?

Meet a wild Pokémon to start a turn-based battle. Weaken it with moves, then choose a Poké Ball from the battle menu to try to catch it.

Which Pokémon game combines platforming with turn-based battles?

Poké-Mario combines classic platforming with catching Pokémon and turn-based battles. This free fan project runs right in your browser.
